R Shiny · Live demos

Interactive risk & modeling apps

Four working R Shiny demos — each one hosted live on shinyapps.io. Click through to launch.

Portfolio Analytics

Portfolio exposure & RWA dashboard

A portfolio-management dashboard to visualize exposure metrics with drill-down (top drivers, historical trending) and period-over-period comparison — illustrating portfolio dynamics, credit-rating migration, and more.

Launch app ↗
Time-Series Forecasting

ARIMA for financial time series

An R Shiny app (built with modularization) for fitting ARIMA models to any financial time series pulled from the Yahoo API. Easily extended to multiple data sources (e.g., Quandl) and frequencies.

Launch app ↗ modularized source ↗
Stress Testing

PPNR / PBIL stress-testing models

An R Shiny app (modularized) illustrating the PRA's PPNR/PBIL stress-testing models based on BoE open data. Supports expert overrides on model specification and parameters, and can accommodate a bank's internal data.

Launch app ↗
Scenario Generation

Economic Scenario Generator

A toy app (R Markdown flexdashboard) for an Economic Scenario Generator (ESG), specified by a Vector Autoregression model built on the chosen macro time series.
